# Baze University FYP Documentation Guidelines

B.Sc. Information Technology final year project template — Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Computing and Applied Science, Baze University, Abuja, Nigeria.

## Document order

1. Title Page
2. Declaration
3. Certification
4. Approval
5. Acknowledgement
6. Dedication
7. Abstract
8. Table of Contents
9. List of Tables
10. List of Figures
11. List of Abbreviations
12. Chapter 1 – Chapter 5 (body)
13. References
14. Appendices

**Page numbering**: Declaration through List of Abbreviations use lowercase roman numerals (iii, iv, v, …). Chapter 1 restarts at Arabic numeral 1.

> Sources: this file merges two supervisor-provided documents — an online template (pasted 2026-09-02) covering document order and the chapter/section breakdown, and a Word doc (`hard_copy_project_instructions.docx`, converted 2026-09-02) covering formatting mechanics, referencing, and submission logistics. Where they overlap, the Word doc's rules were treated as authoritative (see the table/figure numbering note below).

## Abstract

Rules:
- No first-person narrative.
- Single line spacing, no paragraphing.
- Maximum 250 words.
- Must cover: method(s) used to analyze the problem, brief description of research design, key results, significance of results, key conclusions.
- **Write this last**, only after the entire project is complete.

## Table of Contents / List of Tables / List of Figures / List of Abbreviations

- Standard auto-generated TOC listing chapters/sections with page numbers.
- Table and figure captions should be brief and informative.
- **Numbering convention (from the Word doc, resolves an ambiguity in the online template)**: tables and figures are numbered *per chapter*, not sequentially through the whole document — e.g. Chapter 4's tables are Table 4.1, Table 4.2, Table 4.3…, and Chapter 4's figures are Figure 4.1, Figure 4.7, etc. The online template's earlier draft TOC reused "Figure 1" for two different figures (Use Case Diagram and ERD) — that's a leftover mistake from sequential numbering, not something to copy; chapter-scoped numbering avoids the collision naturally.
- List of Abbreviations example entries: CPU (Central Processing Unit), ERD (Entity Relationship Diagram), IT (Information Technology) — extend with whatever abbreviations the actual project uses.

## Typing / formatting instructions

- **Paper**: A4 (21.0 × 29.7cm), typed on one side only.
- **Software/font**: Microsoft Word, Times New Roman.
- **Body text size**: 12pt.
- **Line spacing**: 1.5 throughout the body, **except** the abstract, indented quotations, and footnotes — those are single-spaced.
- **Paragraphing**: block paragraphing — leave one extra blank line between paragraphs (no first-line indent).
- **Margins**: 3.5cm left, 2.5cm top/bottom/right.
- **Heading spacing**: Heading 1 — 12pt before / 8pt after. Heading 2 — 8pt before / 6pt after. Body paragraphs — 6pt after.

## Tables and figures

**Tables**:
- Keep tables brief and clear; very long tables belong in the appendix, not the chapter body.
- Numbered per chapter (Table 4.1, Table 4.2, …), each with a clear, self-explanatory, **bold** title placed **above** the table, close to its body.
- Avoid gridlines (considered an outdated formatting style).
- Content should be centered, and every table must be discussed/referenced in the surrounding text — don't drop one in with no prose pointing to it.

**Figures** (graphs, charts, drawings, diagrams, maps, screenshots — anything graphic that isn't a table):
- Numbered per chapter (Figure 4.7, …).
- Caption goes **below** the figure (opposite of tables, which caption above).
- Must be described/referenced in the body text; cite the source if the figure isn't the student's own work.

## Literature Review requirements

- Chapter 2 should be **at least 25 pages**.
- Include a table summarizing the reviewed literature in detail (source, focus, method, findings/gap — a comparison table, not just prose).

## Plagiarism and AI-content report

- A plagiarism report must be included.
- **Similarity: no more than 20%.**
- **AI-generated content: no more than 10%.**
- Directly relevant to this project: humanize written chapters and keep AI-flagged content under that 10% ceiling — this is a hard submission requirement, not just style preference.

## Submission requirements

- Hard cover colour: **Maroon**.
- Submit **4 hard copies** plus a **CD** containing the project document, codebase, and a description/README.
